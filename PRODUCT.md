# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Self-directed retail investors who plan before they trade. They open Norviq to check positions,
budget and plans, usually at a desk or on a phone after the market closes, and come back through
the weekly digest email to read what changed over the week and what is coming next. The job is to
understand their own money accurately and decide calmly, not to react.

## Product Purpose

Norviq is a financial intelligence system: portfolio tracking, planning tools, budgets, research and
an AI assistant (Q) that work from the user's own data. Success is a user who trusts every number on
screen, finds the answer in seconds, and acts on a plan rather than an impulse.

## Brand Personality

Disciplined, precise, calm. Tagline: "Discipline creates freedom." Voice is calm, declarative and
literal about money: short sentences, no exclamation marks, no hype, no promises about returns. The
interface represents discipline, the data represents reality, and nothing decorative competes with a
number. Canonical identity: `../docs/precision-identity.md`.

## Anti-references

- Robinhood-style gamification: confetti, celebratory animation on trades, streaks that push action.
- Crypto-exchange neon: glow, mesh gradients, decorative circuitry, saturated green/red as decoration.
- SaaS hero-metric dashboards: big number + small label + gradient accent, identical card grids.
- Invented numbers anywhere in the product, including illustrations and marketing captures.

## Design Principles

1. **The number is the hero.** Typography, spacing and color exist to make figures legible and
   comparable (tabular numerals, clear sign, clear unit). Decoration never competes with data.
2. **Literal over clever.** Labels say exactly what a figure is and over which period. Direction is
   never colour alone.
3. **Gold is rare.** The accent marks the one primary action, the active item, focus, or one key
   insight per screen. Gold never means profit.
4. **Calm hierarchy.** One clear entry point per screen, quiet structure around it, no alarmist
   styling for ordinary market moves.
5. **Works with effects off.** Every screen is legible and correct with motion, blur and images off.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG 2.2 AA. Body text ≥4.5:1, large text and controls ≥3:1, in both light and dark themes (light is a
first-class user setting). Every animation has a `prefers-reduced-motion` alternative (opacity only).
Financial direction carries a sign, arrow or accessible label in addition to colour. English and
pt-PT locales; layouts must hold longer Portuguese strings.
